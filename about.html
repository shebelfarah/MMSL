Who We Are

Maisha Medical Supplies Limited is a leading distributor of quality medical products across the East African region. We supply a wide range of pharmaceuticals, medical sundries, medical consumables, and medical furniture to healthcare facilities.

We are committed to supplying high quality pharmaceutical and medical products that exceed customer expectations. Our ability to meet these demands is significantly enhanced through a quality management system which satisfies the requirements of the international standard ISO 9001:2015.

Based in Kampala, Uganda, we serve healthcare providers across Uganda and the Great Lakes region, providing expert consultative guidance on medical products, equipment pricing, and healthcare technology trends.

Our Core Values

The CVD policy drives everything we do — Quality, Value, and Delivery.

Quality

Quality in all products and services we place in the market. We source from certified manufacturers and maintain rigorous quality control.

Value

Delivery

Corporate Social Responsibility

At Maisha Medical Supplies, corporate social responsibility remains an integral part of our commercial philosophy. Our business activities are focused on improving the health and wellbeing of the local communities we serve.

We believe that by providing quality medical supplies at fair prices, we contribute to the overall health infrastructure of the East African region, making healthcare more accessible and affordable for all.
