Our Product Categories

We supply a comprehensive range of medical products to healthcare facilities across East Africa.

Emergency & Resuscitation

Resuscitators, laryngoscopes, oxygen systems, and suction units.

Diagnostic & Monitoring

Stethoscopes, monitors, ECG, ultrasound, and diagnostic sets.

Hospital Furniture & Beds

Hospital beds, trolleys, examination tables, and ward furniture.

Surgical & Sterilization

Autoclaves, sterilizers, holloware, and O.T. lights.
